About Me

I am a first-generation college graduate with a Bachelor of Science in Information Systems, focused on building a long-term career in healthcare data analytics and clinical informatics.

My academic and project experience centers around transforming complex healthcare data into structured, meaningful insights that support operational decision-making and patient care improvement. I have worked with public health datasets from federal sources, designed relational healthcare databases, and performed utilization and cost analyses using SQL and Python.

As someone who is continuously learning and growing, I am committed to strengthening my technical foundation in healthcare data modeling, SQL analytics, and KPI reporting. I am especially interested in hospital operations analytics, clinical quality metrics, and population health trend analysis.

My goal is to contribute to data-driven healthcare environments where analytics supports both organizational efficiency and improved patient outcomes. I am motivated by growth, consistency, and building expertise within healthcare data systems.
